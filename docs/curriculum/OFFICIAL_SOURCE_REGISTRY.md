# Phase 27.5 — Mathematics Official Source Registry

## Scope

Edulytics Phase 27.5 is Mathematics-only and contains exactly four framework families:

1. England / British Mathematics
2. American / Common Core Mathematics
3. UAE Ministry of Education Mathematics
4. Polish National Curriculum Mathematics

No IB, CBSE, French or SABIS pack is part of Phase 27.5.

## England

Primary statutory source:
`https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study`

Post-16 Mathematics source:
`https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gce-as-and-a-level-mathematics`

Reuse basis:
Open Government Licence v3.0, except identified third-party material.

Edulytics keeps the National Curriculum KS1-KS4 source distinct from the post-16 AS/A-level subject-content source.

## Common Core

Official Mathematics source:
`https://corestandards.org/mathematics-standards/`

Official PDF:
`https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Math_Standards1.pdf`

Commercial-use terms reference:
`https://www.thecorestandards.org/commercial-license/`

The Edulytics product owner explicitly confirmed that commercial-use evidence is held. The repository records that fact without fabricating a licence number, submission confirmation or effective date.

Required attribution is represented by the application contract.

K-12 maps to 13 Edulytics logical levels:
Kindergarten = logical 1; Grade 12 = logical 13.

## UAE Ministry of Education

Official authority:
`https://www.moe.gov.ae/`

Current assessment/scope metadata:
`https://www.moe.gov.ae/en/guides/Pages/Student-Assessment-Policy-Guide-2025-2026.aspx`

Historical official curriculum-framework reference:
`https://www.moe.gov.ae/Ar/ImportantLinks/Assessment/Pages/Curriculum-Docs.aspx`

The historical Mathematics Curriculum Standards Framework 2017 reference is versioned as historical provenance. Current assessment policy is not treated as a substitute for detailed curriculum outcomes.

UAE maps Grades 1-12 only. Edulytics must never fabricate Grade 13.

Native upper-secondary pathways are preserved:

- Technikum: five upper-secondary years; Klasa V may map to logical level 13.

## Traceability and content rule

Every imported official item must remain traceable to:
framework, framework version, native grade/year/pathway, Mathematics subject,
source authority, source URL/source identifier, source version/effective period,
reuse/attribution basis, and official/deterministic item identifier where available.

Edulytics must not invent official standards or learning outcomes.

The repository does not vendor full official-source snapshots. The verification tool downloads official sources to `/tmp/edulytics-phase275-official-sources`, hashes them and validates provenance at acceptance time.
